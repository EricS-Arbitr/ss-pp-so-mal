Service Area

Five townships, 312 square miles of western Pennsylvania.

Townships served

Voltgrid Township — ZIP 16001
Headquarters, original generating station, and main customer-care facility. Service since 1923.
Powerton — ZIP 16002
Added 1947 after the Allegheny interconnection. Largest concentration of light industrial accounts.
Currentville — ZIP 16003
Added 1956. Service operations center and primary line-crew dispatch.
Wattford — ZIP 16004
Added 1962. Hosts the largest of Voltgrid's three substations and a 22 MW community solar field commissioned in 2021.
Ampere Heights — ZIP 16005
Added 1971. Most recent annexation and largest residential growth area.

Outside our service area?

Voltgrid does not operate beyond the five-township footprint above. If you're building or moving to an adjacent area, your provider is most likely Allegheny Light & Power or a regional cooperative. We're happy to help you identify your local utility — call 1-800-VOLTGRID.
