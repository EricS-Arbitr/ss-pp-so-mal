Services

Residential, commercial, and solar service across our five-township footprint.

Residential

Standard household service, billed monthly. Flat $0.13/kWh + $12.50 monthly fee. Online account management and autopay through the Customer Portal.

New connections typically complete within 5 business days. Same-day reconnection available for moves within our service footprint.

Commercial & Industrial

Over 3,200 commercial accounts from corner stores to mid-sized manufacturing. Demand-charge or flat-rate plans, time-of-use options, three-phase service throughout our footprint.

Dedicated account managers for customers above 50 kW peak demand.

Solar & Battery Interconnection

Grid-tied solar arrays certified under PA Net Metering rule 52 Pa. Code § 75. Studies for systems under 50 kW typically complete within 30 days.

Battery storage systems are eligible for the Voltgrid Resilience Credit ($0.04/kWh for grid-supportive discharge windows).

Energy Assistance

Voltgrid participates in the Pennsylvania LIHEAP program and operates the Voltgrid Lifeline Fund, a community-funded hardship program for customers facing temporary disconnection due to income loss, medical emergencies, or natural disasters.

Call 1-800-VOLTGRID and ask for the Lifeline desk.

Outage reporting

For active outages, call 1-800-VOLTGRID or report through the Customer Portal. Outage maps are updated every 5 minutes during active events.

If you smell burning insulation, see arcing, or see a downed line, leave the area immediately and call 911 first, then us.
