About Voltgrid Power Co.

A regional utility, owned by the communities we serve, since 1923.

Voltgrid Power Company was chartered on April 14, 1923, when a small group of industrialists and farm cooperatives in western Pennsylvania pooled their resources to build a coal-fired plant on the Allegheny tributary. The first 800 homes were lit that December.

A hundred and three years later, we serve roughly 84,000 residential and 3,200 commercial accounts across five townships and the surrounding rural service area. We remain a regional utility — not part of any national holding company — and our generation, transmission, and distribution operations are still run from our headquarters in Voltgrid Township.

Our mission

Deliver dependable electric service at a fair price, invest steadily in cleaner generation, and respect the communities we operate in. Every line crew, customer-care agent, and engineer at Voltgrid is empowered to make decisions that keep service moving when something goes wrong.
